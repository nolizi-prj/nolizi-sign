### 2. Intent Statement Alignment ([`L-005`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi/lessons/L-005-review-checks-coherence-not-intent.md))
[`INTENT.md`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-sign/spec/0006/INTENT.md) identifies three critical integrity issues where terminal envelopes could previously be modified and audited:
1. Voiding/cancelling executed or terminated envelopes.
2. CC signers re-running completion machinery and stamping a second `completed` audit event.
3. Signers declining after signing, declining finished envelopes, or declining out of turn.

[`SPEC.md`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-sign/spec/0006/SPEC.md) precisely addresses all three defects while adhering to the declared non-goals in `INTENT.md`:
- No new mechanism to void completed agreements on purpose.
- Expiration logic (`expired` status) is preserved for `BACKLOG.md` item 2.
- Out-of-scope boundaries are respected (`backend/`, `frontend/`, `CLAUDE.md`, and deployment remain untouched).

- **Database Immutability on Refusal:** Every refusal path reads back database rows to ensure neither `submissions` nor `submitters` was modified.
- **Boundary of `expired`:** Documented in §S5. Because the worker currently never writes `expired` (tracked in A-409), omitting `expired` from `isTerminal` avoids guarding an unreachable state until `BACKLOG.md` item 2 is implemented.
- **PDF Stamping Boundary:** A-406 proves that `finalize()` was not re-executed via timestamp immutability and single audit event count, acknowledging in §S5 that R2 binary re-stamping is skipped in this suite due to no-PDF test seeding.
