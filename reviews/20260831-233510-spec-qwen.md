However, there is a direct internal inconsistency in the amendment rationale. `SPEC.md` §S5a says, as part of arguing that the rename is not a deletion and is best classified as a fixture/string-location change:

> “Nothing was deleted: it is one `git mv`, the file's content is byte-identical, and the suite that runs it is the same suite.”

But `SPEC.md` §S5 says the renamed file’s `test()` title is changed and a header comment is added:

> “and the `test()` title from … to … **Not one assertion changed**; a header comment was added…”

Those statements cannot both be true of the final file content. The accurate claim appears to be that the assertions/behavior are unchanged, not that the file content is byte-identical. Because §S5a uses the byte-identical claim to support the amendment’s classification and safety argument, this needs correction in the spec.

The spec’s stated claim is narrower, so I am not citing those as the objection. The M4 redundancy is also disclosed rather than concealed, so I am not treating it as an L-006 failure.
