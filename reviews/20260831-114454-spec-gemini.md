## 3. Acceptance Case Invariants & Falsifiability ([`L-006`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi/lessons/L-006-tests-that-cannot-fail.md))

All six acceptance cases in [`frontend/src/landing-claims.spec.ts`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-sign/frontend/src/landing-claims.spec.ts) and [`frontend/e2e/landing-page.spec.ts`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-sign/frontend/e2e/landing-page.spec.ts) contain deterministic, unconditional assertions directly coupled to their named specification clauses:

3. **A-003 (`S2a`):**

   - "Guards the guard" by verifying that `$25` exists in `MARKET_MD` prose but is absent from `MARKET_PRICE_ROWS`, ensuring whole-file refutation text cannot mask unbacked figures.
4. **A-004 (`S2b`, `S2c`):**

5. **A-005 (`S3`):** Asserts exact byte preservation of the three shipped Apache-2.0 strings at `10a523d` until Q-021 resolution.
6. **A-006 (`S1b`, `S1d`, `S2a`):** End-to-end browser test verifying that the rendered badge matches `STAGE.md`, that the painted DOM contains no conflicting stage words, and that rendered table prices appear in `MARKET.md` pricing table rows.
