## 2. Intent Statement Alignment ([`L-005`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi/lessons/L-005-review-checks-coherence-not-intent.md))

[`INTENT.md`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-sign/spec/0006/INTENT.md) identifies three critical integrity issues where terminal envelopes could be modified and audited post-completion:
1. Voiding/cancelling executed or terminated envelopes.
2. CC signers re-triggering `finalize()` on completed envelopes.
3. Signers declining after signing, declining finished envelopes, or declining out of turn.

[`SPEC.md`](file:///home/m/dev/pumasi-sign/spec/0006/SPEC.md) accurately specifies fixes for all three defects (§S2a–§S2c) while strictly honoring the non-goals defined in `INTENT.md`:
- No new mechanism to void completed agreements on purpose.
- Expiration logic (`expired` status) is deliberately excluded (§S5) and preserved for `BACKLOG.md` item 2.
- Scope bounds (§S5, §S6) are preserved (`backend/`, `frontend/`, and deployment remain untouched).

## 4. Edge Cases & Boundary Conditions

- **Order-Independent Audit Invariants:** Audit assertions use multiset comparisons (sorting by event type) or snapshot comparisons, avoiding race conditions on millisecond timestamps (`L-006`).
- **State Survives Refusals:** Every refused transition is immediately followed by database row reads to confirm the record was not modified.
- **Exclusion of `expired`:** Documented in §S5. The worker currently lacks a scheduled cron sweep (recorded in A-409); omitting `expired` from `isTerminal` avoids guarding against an unreachable state until `BACKLOG.md` item 2 introduces scheduled transitions.
- **PDF Stamping Boundary:** A-406 proves that `finalize()` was not re-executed via `completed_at` immutability and single audit event count, acknowledging in §S5 that R2 binary re-stamping is skipped in this suite due to no-PDF test seeding.
